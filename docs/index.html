Built for how you naturally speak.

Designed from scratch to deliver the speed of thought without privacy compromise or subscription paywalls.

0ms Push-to-Talk

Global low-level keyboard hooks (Caps Lock or Ctrl). Holds pre-roll audio so the very first syllable is never lost.

Hindi, English & Hinglish

Flawless multilingual understanding. Automatic Devanagari or Roman phonetic script switcher for casual Hinglish conversations.

85Hz Noise Suppression

Built-in 2nd-order Butterworth high-pass filter strips fan noise and room hum, with adaptive VAD for crystal-clear inference.

Zero Login & 100% Private

No account creation, no signups, no analytics. Use free cloud Whisper models or run 100% offline with your local GPU.

Floating Scratchpad & Notes

Quickly jot down stream-of-consciousness ideas without switching windows or interrupting your active workflow.

Meeting Summarizer

Records discussions, highlights key decisions, and automatically structures action items with time-stamped audio replay.

Three simple steps to flow.

No complicated setup or configurations. Just install and speak.

01

Hold the Key

Press and hold Caps Lock or Ctrl anywhere in any window.

02

Speak Freely

Speak in Hindi, English, or mixed Hinglish. The floating pill visualizes your voice with live audio waveforms.

03

Instant Auto-Paste

Release the key. Your thoughts are transcribed by Whisper Large v3 and pasted straight into your active input field!
